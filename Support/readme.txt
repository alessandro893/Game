Installation Instructions:

Exit all applications. It is recommend that you do not run unnecessary 
background applications when installing the game.

Insert Disc 1 into your computers CD drive.  The Autorun menu will 
appear as soon as your disk drive recognizes the disk.  Select to 
Install the game.  A prompt will appear to enter your install code.  
Enter the install code and click on the Next button.

You will then get a menu asking to choose either a default install or 
the advanced install (which allows you to specify an install 
directory).  Select Next and the game will begin installing.

VIDEO CARD SUPPORT
==================

Although Direct3D is supported, this support is only officially 
extended to certain 3D accelerator devices.  The following major D3D 
chipsets are supported

ATI Radeon 7500 or greater
Intel i865
Matrox Parhelia
NVidia geForce2 or greater
SiS Xabre 400

While Direct3D supports a wider variety of 3D accelerator devices, the 
behavior of NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND cannot be predicted in these 
situations.  In extreme cases, running NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND with 
a Direct3D device that is not officially supported can lead to an 
unstable PC environment.

Under Direct3D, the performance of NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND (in both 
graphics AND audio) is influenced greatly by the Direct3D driver 
versions that are installed.  It is highly recommended that the latest 
versions of all video card and sound card drivers be obtained and 
installed.  Please consult your hardware manufacturer for their newest 
drivers. Also, keep in mind that video chip manufacturers sometimes 
provide drivers in addition to the card manufacturer.

DEFAULT GRAPHICS SETTINGS
==============================
The first time NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND is run, a system check is 
performed and default levels are set for the various graphics 
settings. These default levels are set to provide the best game play 
experience possible based on the hardware detected in your system.

These settings can be adjusted in the Display menu of the Options. In 
the Display options, choose Configure Graphics Card.

Choosing Configure Graphics Card takes you to the Graphics menu with 
four options:

Resolution: Use the arrows to increase or decrease the game 
resolution. Increasing the game’s resolution may slow down system 
performance.

Details: Use the plus or minus signs on the Details bar to increase or 
decrease global graphics setting. Increasing the Details setting to 
high will improve the game’s graphics but may slow down system 
performance.

Customize Display Settings: Tweak individual graphics settings. (See 
below.)

Default Settings: Reset your graphics settings to the default for your 
hardware configuration.

Selecting Customize Display Settings in the graphics menu takes you to 
the Customize Display Settings menu. The following individual settings 
are available to tweak to change the game’s graphics output and game 
performance. Turning all the settings to on may slow down system 
performance.

Car Reflection Update Rate: Use the plus and minus at either end of 
the bar to increase or decrease how quickly reflections visible on the 
car update.

Car Reflection Detail: Setting this to High increases the detail of 
reflections visible on the car.

Car Geometry Detail: Setting this to High increases geometry details 
on the car.

World Environment Map: Turn on or off world environment mapping.

Car Shadow: Moving this slider to the right increases car shadows. By 
moving the slider to the left, the amount of car shadows are decreased
which may improve system performance.

Car Headlight: Turn on or off headlights.

Crowds: Turn on or off animated crowd characters.

Road Reflection Detail: Use the plus and minus at either end of the 
bar to increase or decrease reflections visible in the road.

World Detail: Moving this slider to the right lets you to increase the 
amount of detail visible in world geometry. Decreasing world geometry 
detail (by moving the slider to the left) may increase system 
performance.

Light Trails: Turn on or off light trails created as you drive by 
lights in the world.

Light Glow: Turn on or off the glow on light sources.

Animated Texture: Turn on or off texture animations.

Particle System: Turn on or off particle effects.

Motion Blur: Turn on or off motion blur.

Not all graphic details are available for all video cards due to 
hardware limitations.

ADDITIONAL CAR SELECT FUNCTIONALITY
===================================
While on the car select screen, the car can be rotated by left 
clicking with the mouse and moving in the desired direction.

When changing cars, the new car will appear in the default position, 
but can be moved as desired.

DRIVER PROFILE AND SAVE LOAD
============================

Driver Profile: When you first start NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND, you 
will be prompted to create a Driver Profile. This is a save file for 
your global options and user data.

If you are in the Main Menu and you want to create a new Driver 
Profile,press BACKSPACE. This logs out your current Driver Profile and 
lets you make a new one.

When you start a new driver profile, you will not have any of the
progress (unlocked cars, parts, or progress through Underground Mode)
that you had in your old profile. (You can always log back in under
your original profile if you want to go back.)

Manual Saves: Your game can be manually saved by accessing the Save 
LoadMenu. This is found by going Main Menu >> Options >> Save / Load 
>> Save.

There are 8 slots available for saves.

NOTE: A save game does not effect your driver profile. Instead, it 
saves your game progress. When you make a save game, you are saving 
your progress in Underground Mode, the number of cars, car upgrades, 
and tracks you have unlocked, and any modifications you have made to 
your cars.

You cannot import save games from one Driver Profile to use with
a different Driver Profile.

Manual Loads: Your game progress can be manually loaded by going 
Main Menu >> Options >> Save / Load >> Load. Any manual saves you
have made are listed here.

The final game save directory is located at:
\Documents and Settings\All Users\Application Data\NFS Underground

4.			       TROUBLESHOOTING

************************************************************


INSTALLING TO THE CORRECT FOLDER
======================================
When installing NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND for the first time, please 
make sure that you have selected/created and highlighted the exact 
directory folder you want to install to. Different versions of Windows 
default to new folders in slightly different ways so please 
double-check that you have specified the intended location.


CD AUTOPLAY FEATURE
===================
Inserting the game CD into your CD-ROM drive and then immediately 
running the game either from the Windows Start menu or by 
double-clicking the game's Desktop icon may cause a conflict with the 
CD-ROM's AutoPlay feature. The easiest way to avoid this potential 
conflict is to always wait a few moments after inserting the CD to 
allow the AutoPlay feature to run the game.


EJECTING THE GAME CD
====================
Never eject the game CD when the game is running.


DEVICE DRIVERS
==============
It is highly recommended that the latest versions of all device 
drivers (i.e. video and sound card drivers) be obtained and installed.
Please consult your card manufacturer for their newest drivers. Also, 
keep in mind that video chip manufacturers sometimes provide drivers 
in addition to the card manufacturer.  
If you are experiencing issues with an unstable PC environment please 
make sure your Motherboard drivers (AGP, miniport) are up to date. For 
further information please contact your Motherboard manufacture.

VIDEO & GRAPHICS PROBLEMS
=========================
If you are experiencing performance problems on the default graphics 
settings, try lowering some of the settings or the game resolution.

This game requires DirectX 9.0
It is available for download by going to:

Directx 9.0		www.microsoft.com/windows/directx/

NOTE:
nVidia Detonator 44.03 Driver
Game stability issues have been found when playing NEED FOR SPEED 
UNDERGROUND with the nVidia 44.03 driver (commonly found on GeForce2, 
GeForce3 and GeForce4 video cards) and running in a Windows 98/ME 
environment. If you have a GeForce card and are experiencing game 
crashes or other instability, please update to the most recent nVidia 
driver.

There are some known issues with the ATI Catalyst 3.8 drivers on 
higher end Radeon cards. If NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND fails to run or 
graphical anomalies occur using these drivers, try reverting back to 
ATI Catalyst 3.7 drivers.

AUDIO & SOUND PROBLEMS
======================

INSTALLED SOUND CARD, BUT THERE IS NO SOUND
*Make sure your speakers or headphones are plugged into the 
appropriate jack and the volume control is turned up. If this does 
not solve the problem make sure your sound card is installed 
correctly.

BACKGROUND PROGRAMS
===================
Avoid running other programs in the background.

Programs starting up automatically can cause NEED FOR SPEED 
UNDERGROUND to lose control of the screen and keyboard, possibly 
causing an unstable PC environment.  We strongly recommend that you 
shut down or suspend applications that start up automatically or 
programs that automatically run other applications at specified times 
before running NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND.  These include, but are not 
limited to:
	- Windows System Agent
  	- Windows Task Scheduler
	- Windows "Disk Cleanup" utilities

Similarly, message boxes and dialog boxes from other programs or from 
Windows itself can also cause NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND to lose 
control of the screen and keyboard, possibly causing an unstable PC 
environment. We strongly recommend that you shut down all other 
applications before running NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND.  These 
include, but are not limited to:
  	- Email programs
	- Calendar, appointment, or contact applications
	- Network or Internet chat programs
	- Automatic Update on Anti Virus software

Also, connections to/from non-dedicated servers can be disrupted when 
background programs or screen-savers launch.


POWER MANAGEMENT SUSPEND/SLEEP MODES
============================================
Systems (i.e. laptops or other portables) that automatically power 
down into "sleep" or "suspended" modes can cause NEED FOR SPEED 
UNDERGROUND to lose control of the screen and keyboard, possibly 
causing an unstable PC environment. We strongly recommend that you 
disable these features before running NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND.

Also, connections to/from non-dedicated servers can be disrupted when 
sleep or suspended modes occur.


ALT+TAB FUNCTIONALITY 
=========================
ALT+TAB will switch out of NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND, but the game 
does not automatically pause. If you are going to do this, pause 
manually before swapping windows.

5.			       ONLINE PLAY

************************************************************



NOTE: NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND supports only broadband connections 
for online play. When logging in to play Online you are logging into 
the EA.com servers. If you already have one, you may use your existing 
online EA.com account to play or you can create a new one.

Setting up an account: An EA account is required for online play. At 
the account log-in screen choose Create New EA Account. You will be 
taken to menus where you enter account information and choose your 
online user name.

NOTE: If your existing EA account contains a space, you won’t be able
to use it due to a limitation in the game.

You can have up to four EA Online User Names. User Names can also be 
deleted if you would like to change one.

Starting a game online: After you log in, you have the choice to pick 
a car. All the cars that you have unlocked for single player Quick 
Race mode are available for play online. Some tuned sponsor cars will 
also be available but they may not be customized or used in ranked 
races. 

Main Online Menu
================

Options available in the Main Online Menu are:

Ranked Game: Every race counts toward your online ranking.

Unranked Game: Unranked online races do not affect your ranking.
Select Car: Switch cars for the next race.

News: Catch up on the latest NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND news, or read 
the membership agreement.

Rankings: See the top 100 online racers. Stats are shown for 
each race mode as well as overall leaders and the week’s best track 
times.

NOTE: If you have a Disconnect rate greater than 25% you won’t be 
officially ranked for that event. 

Options: Change gameplay settings

Game Mode Select
================

After you have chosen to play either a Ranked or Unranked game,
the Game Mode Select screen comes up. Here, you must pick a room 
to race in.

Use the arrow buttons to change race modes and highlight a room to 
race in. From this screen, you can see how many people are currently 
in each room. Press ENTER to enter a highlighted room. Press the Z 
button to create your own room.

Room Menu: Each race room displays information about the racers within 
it. From this menu you can look at other players’ stats, send race 
challenges, and chat.

Rep: Reputation points. The more you play Ranked games and win, the 
more reputation points you have. Playing ranked games and losing will 
cause your reputation to decrease.

Connection: Players with more green hashes have better connections 
to the game server.

To chat to everyone in the room, click the chat bar and start typing. 
Press ENTER to send the message.

To bring up the challenge menu, select the user you’d like to 
challenge and press ENTER.

Head-To-Head Challenge Menu: After you select a user you would like 
to challenge, you are taken to this menu.

Send Challenge:	Pick the track and the number of laps you 
would like for your challenge race. To challenge another racer 
to a one-on-one race, highlight their name, press ENTER and select 
Send Challenge. Select CANCEL to cancel challenge.

Challenge Options
=================

View Player Details: Study another player’s stats. Block Player 
Prevent individual players from sending you challenges.

View Car: Have a look at the highlighted player’s car.

Register Complaint: Used to capture chat log and send it to EA 
Online Services to report abuse of Membership agreement. 

PRIVATE MESSAGE: Sends a message to the highlighted player. 
(You can also send a quick private message by clicking the chat bar 
and typing "/tell Playername" followed by the message.)

To switch to Created Games List, press right arrow button. 
To return to the Head to Head Challenge List press the left arrow 
button.

Press Z to create a game or e to view/join a previously created game.

Create a four-player game and wait for additional players to join. 
Select START GAME to begin.

GENERAL ONLINE INFO
===================
Frame Rate: If your frame rate dips below 12 fps for 10 consecutive 
frames or longer, you you should receive the message “Frame Rate is 
too low for a Ranked Game.” Lowering your graphic detail settings may 
help increase your framerate so you can play in ranked games.

6.			       CONTROLLER INFORMATION

************************************************************

CONTROLLER CONFIGURATIONS 
================================
NEED FOR SPEED UNDERGROUND can be played with many popular gamepads 
and controller devices. If your device is not recognized, or if you'd 
like to modify the configuration the game has pre-assigned to your 
controller, enter Controller Options from Options on the Main Menu. 
The re-mapping tools in this menu are useful for tweaking your 
controller for optimal play.

JOYSTICK PROBLEMS
=======================
Any joystick supported by Windows will work with NEED FOR SPEED 
UNDERGROUND.
However, if the device is not properly configured in Windows, then 
erratic behavior may occur.  Such symptoms include, but are not 
limited to:

- The mouse pointer automatically scrolls through the buttons on menu 
screens.
- Your car makes erratic movements that are not intended (i.e. 
continually turning).
- Certain movements do not occur when the proper direction is pressed.
- Sluggish response on slower machines.

To correct these problems, properly configure and calibrate the device 
from the Windows Control Panel.  If problems continue to persist, 
disconnect the device from your computer before playing NEED FOR SPEED 
UNDERGROUND Ensure the latest manufacturer drivers are installed for 
your device.. 

FORCE FEEDBACK SETTINGS
=============================
Force Feedback is not enabled on the Customize Controller screen of 
the Options menu until proper, device-specific drivers are installed 
for that controller. The default generic drivers provided by Windows 
do not provide access to Force Feedback features.


KEYBOARD PROBLEMS
=======================
To adjust the feeling\sensitivity of the steering in gameplay when 
using the keyboard, enter the Keyboard option in the Windows Control 
Panel (Start - Settings - Control Panel). By adjusting the Repeat 
Delay and Repeat Rate the user can adjust the controls to feel more 
analog or digital depending on their preference. 

Some newer keyboards do not allow certain combinations of more than 
two keys to be depressed simultaneously.  For example, if you were 
pressing both the up arrow key to accelerate and the left arrow key to 
turn, and you pressed the <END> key to change your view, view may not 
change.  Unfortunately, this problem is due to a limitation of the 
hardware.
